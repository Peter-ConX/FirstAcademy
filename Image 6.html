Why Elementary Entrepreneurship Education?

We humans live by inventions everyday of our lives, and without inventors and builders of brands around the world who continue to produce the phones, computers, mobile applications, home appliances, air conditioners, airplanes, shoes, wristwatches, perfumes plus the jobs that come with these creations, there will be no human civilization. In other words, we live in a world powered by inventions and brain power. To that extent, no subject ought to be more important in any school curriculum, national development plan, pro education CSR anywhere on earth or in any religion than the subject of invention for primary and secondary school children, because at the end of the day, all prosperity, personal or national, exist because an inventor invented value which turned into jobs and goods and exports. So why not deliberately allow Nigeria overflow with a new population of jobs and wealth creators whose brain become ignited in value invention and problem solving early enough.
